On flight sites

From search result to Sabre in one click

Works on ITA Matrix, Google Flights, Kayak, CheapOair, FlyBasis, PointsYeah and AwardLogic.

GDS copies the option

Every leg in Sabre *IA and VI* format, with equipment, mileage, cabin and date changes filled in. Paste it into the terminal as is.

Lead ID adds it to a lead

Type the 6-digit lead number and the option is added to that lead in the BO with its price, or miles and taxes for award seats. Passengers are counted from the lead.

Sell price set for you

The Sell box fills in Net + 10%, rounded to end in 88 to 92. Type your own price instead, or untick it to leave the sell price at $0.00.

Checks before it adds

If the route doesn't match the lead, it asks first. Unsupported mileage programs are flagged before anything is sent.

In the BO

Building options takes fewer steps

Sort Me

Paste flights in any order. It puts the segments in travel order, picks the carrier, and can move through the Add option steps for you.

Merge

Select two one-ways and press Merge. They become one option, each on its own ticket with its own cost.

Edit the Sell price in place

Click a Sell amount, type the new price and press Enter. It's saved and checked against the BO.

Search the lead in one click

Google, ELR, Matrix, PointsYeah, Basis and Kayak buttons, already filled with the lead's route, dates, cabin and passengers. Open all opens every site you tick, and ↻ re-reads the lead.

Safety checks

A red tag marks options that change airports during a connection, and a warning stops you before sending a quote that shows $0.00.

Daily follow-up

Your call list, one click per lead

Call dials the next lead

When a call ends, the next lead is dialed after a short countdown. Press Stop at any time. If you take another call yourself, it waits.

Double-dial and Redial

Double-dial calls an unanswered lead a second time. Redial calls the last number again whenever you want.

See where you are

Every lead gets a dot by its ID, and the panel shows your place in the list. The day resets at 2pm GMT. Once every lead is done, round 2 begins.

Texts in the RC tab

See the lead's RingCentral texts and reply from the BO, for any country's phone number. Four follow-up templates fill in your name, the client's name and their destination, and you can edit them. The next one to send is highlighted.

Install

Set up in about two minutes

On your work computer, in Chrome.

Add Tampermonkey to Chrome

Find Tampermonkey in the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome.

Remove any older version

If you already have an older copy of this tool or a similar one, delete it in the Tampermonkey dashboard first, or you'll see doubled buttons.

Click Install Ian's Assistant

Use the button at the top of this page. Tampermonkey opens its install screen; click Install.

If nothing happens

Open chrome://extensions, click Details on Tampermonkey, turn on Allow user scripts, then click the install button again.

Open a lead

Pip appears in the bottom-right corner of the BO and says hi with your name.

How do updates work?They install by themselves. When a new version is out you'll also see a small banner; click Update to get it right away.
