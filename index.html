How to install

Ready in about two minutes

One time, on your work computer, in Google Chrome.

  1. 01

    Add Tampermonkey

    Install the free Tampermonkey extension from the Chrome Web Store.

    Open Web Store
  2. 02

    Remove old copies

    If you had an older copy of this tool or a similar one, delete it from the Tampermonkey dashboard first, so you don't see doubled buttons.

  3. 03

    Install TBC Assistant

    Tap the button below. Tampermonkey opens its install page: click Install.

    Install now
  4. 04

    Open a lead

    Open any lead in the BO. Pip appears in the bottom-right corner and greets you by name. That's it.

Nothing happened in step 3? Open chrome://extensions, click Details on Tampermonkey and turn on Allow user scripts. Then tap Install again.

Questions

Good to know

Still stuck? The full list of buttons explains every one.

Is it really free?

Yes. TBC Assistant and Tampermonkey are both free. There is nothing to sign up for.

How do I get updates?

They install by themselves. Within a few minutes of a new version you'll also see a small banner saying it's available: click Update to get it right away.

The What's new button on the panel shows what the latest update changed.

Does it send anything without me?

No. Send quotes shows you every email first and waits for you to press Send, Skip or Stop. Quote cards and copied flights only go where you paste them.

Does it work on my phone?

It is made for Chrome on your work computer, where you use the BO. You can read this page on your phone and install it later from your desk.

I see every button twice.

You have two copies installed. Open the Tampermonkey dashboard, keep TBC Assistant and delete the older one.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. The moon button at the top of the panel opens a menu with five looks: Nova (the main look, like this website), Nova Night, Dark, Light and Navy. Your choice is saved.

Are my settings kept after an update?

Yes. Callbacks, templates, your number and your look all stay. Your follow-up progress is also backed up every day.

Install once, and Pip is waiting in the corner of every lead. Updates arrive on their own.
